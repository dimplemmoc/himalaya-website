Kinnaur

Take the Long Way Through Kinnaur
→

Stop at the little villages along the way, drive past apple orchards, meet the locals and take in the changing landscapes. Kinnaur is the kind of place where you'll probably want to stop more often than you planned.

Valley of Flowers

Worth Every Step
→

It's a proper walk to get there, but the valley makes you forget how far you've come. Come during the flowering season, take your time on the trail and enjoy a side of the Himalayas you won't find on an ordinary road trip.

Garhwal

A Little More Time in the Mountains
→

There's more to Garhwal than its famous destinations. Spend time in its villages, explore quieter roads, try local food and discover places you might not have heard about before.

Char Dham

A Journey to Remember
→

The early starts, the mountain roads, the temple visits and the people you meet along the way. Char Dham is a journey with many moments, and everyone returns with their own experience of it.
