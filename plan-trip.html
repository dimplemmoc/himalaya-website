GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before you go?

Here are a few helpful details about starting your trip inquiry.

How do I start planning my Himalayan trip?

Fill in the trip planner with your preferred destination, dates, group size, budget and travel style. Submit the form to open a WhatsApp message with your details.

Can I submit the form if I haven't chosen a destination?

Yes. Choose "Not decided yet" in the destination list. You can also describe the kind of experience you want in the additional details box.

Can I plan a family trip or a group getaway?

Yes. Select the number of travelers in the form and share any group-specific preferences in the message box.

Does submitting the form confirm my booking?

No. Submitting the form opens WhatsApp with a prepared inquiry. Your trip is not booked or confirmed by submitting this form.
