Everything you need to know about our Himalayan journal, travel stories, and local stays.

Our journal is a curated collection of local stories, indigenous traditions, slow-travel guides and homestay experiences from across Himachal, Uttarakhand, Ladakh and Kashmir. It is crafted to give travellers an authentic understanding of mountain life before they set foot on the trails.

Every story and stay is born out of direct, personal relationships with Himalayan families, village panchayats, and local guides. We visit each location, stay in family homestays, and ensure that community values and ecological ethics are respected.

Spring (April – June) is ideal for pleasant weather and blooming fruit orchards. Autumn (September – November) offers the clearest mountain panoramas, golden foliage, and crisp skies perfect for stargazing. Winter (December – February) is magical for quiet snowscapes and traditional hearthside meals.

We work exclusively with local hosts, homestay owners, regional cooks, and village guides. 100% of accommodation and guiding fees go directly to the mountain communities, ensuring sustainable livelihoods while preventing overtourism in fragile ecosystems.
